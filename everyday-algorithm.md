# Everyday Algorithm: Calculate Shopping Cost

## Problem

Calculate the total cost of items purchased.

## Algorithm

1. Start.
2. Enter the price of one item.
3. Enter the quantity purchased.
4. Multiply the price by the quantity.
5. Display the total cost.
6. Stop.

## Input

- Price per item
- Quantity

## Process

Total cost = Price × Quantity

## Output

The total cost of the items.
